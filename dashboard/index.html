How the value is scored

impliedValue(tvl, growth7d, monthsNoToken, chainCount) — TVL times a size multiple (0.55 under $100m, 0.38 to $1b, 0.26 above), times a growth factor from the 7-day TVL change, times an age factor of +1.8% per month with no token recorded (capped at 36 months), times a breadth factor of +1.2% per chain (capped at 12). No name is ever scored by hand: the same four numbers always give the same value. Pick any row to see its four factors. Board eligibility follows the snapshot’s own watchlist rule: no CEX, bridge, liquid (re)staking or restaked-BTC product, no versioned or wrapped/staked name, no exchange-named protocol — an exchange’s balances are not a launch waiting to happen.
